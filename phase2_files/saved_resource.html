1.Brainstorming Report

The brainstorming process focused on improving the safety, reliability, and lifespan of electric vehicle (EV) batteries. The team identified common battery-related problems such as overheating, overcharging, deep discharge, abnormal current, cell imbalance, inaccurate state-of-charge estimation, and lack of real-time warning. The following ideas were considered:

  • Real-time voltage and current monitoring
  • Battery temperature monitoring and overheating alert
  • State of Charge (SoC) estimation
  • State of Health (SoH) estimation
  • Over-voltage and under-voltage protection
  • Over-current and abnormal-current detection
  • Cell balancing indication
  • IoT/cloud-based remote monitoring
  • Mobile/dashboard display for battery parameters
  • Emergency warning when unsafe battery conditions are detected

4.Proposed Solution

The proposed Smart Electric Vehicle Battery Health Monitoring System is an embedded monitoring unit that continuously measures important battery parameters. Voltage and current sensors measure electrical conditions, a temperature sensor monitors battery temperature, and a microcontroller processes the sensor data. The system estimates SoC and identifies unsafe operating conditions. A display or IoT dashboard can show real-time information, while a buzzer/notification can provide warnings.

5.Basic functional flow:

EV Battery → Sensors → Microcontroller → Data Processing/SoC Estimation → Display/IoT Dashboard → Warning/Alert
